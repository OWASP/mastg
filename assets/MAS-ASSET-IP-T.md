id: MAS-ASSET-IP-T
title: Intellectual Property Data in Transit
---

This asset type covers intellectual property data that is transmitted between the application and a remote endpoint, or between processes on the device via interprocess communication (IPC).

## Examples

- API keys, secrets, or certificates sent in request headers or bodies when the application authenticates itself to its own backends or to third-party services.
- Key material exchanged during key agreement or key transport.
- Secrets passed to other apps or components through intents, content providers, or other IPC channels.
