---
id: MAS-ASSET-IP-R
title: Intellectual Property Data at Rest
---

This asset type covers intellectual property data that is stored persistently on the device, such as in files, databases, shared preferences, or the keychain/keystore.

## Examples

- API keys, secrets, or certificates stored on the device that the application uses to authenticate itself to its own backends or to third-party services.
- App-wide encryption keys or other key material owned by the organization and persisted on the device.
- Credentials embedded in the application binary or its configuration files.
