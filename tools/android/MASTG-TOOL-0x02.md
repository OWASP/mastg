[Undetected Frida](https://github.com/zer0def/undetected-frida "Undetected Frida") publishes Frida releases with community anti-detection patches applied (e.g. renaming Frida-specific strings, sockets, and threads) to make instrumentation harder to detect by apps with Frida counter-measures. It follows Frida upstream and includes installers such as a Magisk module for rooted devices.

Refer to @MASTG-TOOL-0001 for installation and usage details.
